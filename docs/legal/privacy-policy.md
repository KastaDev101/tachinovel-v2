TachiNovel is a reader app for web novels with built-in narration. It is made by an individual
developer, [name], referred to below as "I" or "me". This policy explains what happens to your
information when you use the app.

## The short version

- **No account.** You never sign up or log in to anything run by me.
- **I don't collect your data.** I run no server for the app. Your library, reading progress, history
  and settings stay on your device (and in your own iCloud, if you use it).
- **I don't sell or share your data**, and the app does not track you across other companies' apps or
  websites.
- **Narration runs on your device.** Chapters are read aloud by voices on your iPhone; their text is not
  sent anywhere to be spoken.

## What the app stores on your device

| Data | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Your library, categories, reading progress, history, bookmarks and settings | So the app works and remembers where you were | On your device; small files also sync through your iCloud account if iCloud Drive is on |
| Chapters you download, cached chapters and cover images | Offline reading and speed | On your device only; you can clear them in Settings › Storage |
| Sources and repositories you add | To browse and read from them | On your device (and your iCloud, as above) |
| An app log and crash/hang reports (see "Diagnostics") | To find and fix problems | On your device only |

Backups you export are files you control: they go wherever you choose to save or send them.

iCloud sync is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. I can't see your iCloud data.

## Websites the app connects to

TachiNovel doesn't host books. When you browse, search, open a novel or read a chapter, the app connects
**directly** to the website of the source you chose, the way a web browser would. That website receives
the usual information any website receives: your IP address, the request (for example which page was
asked for) and technical headers, and it may set cookies, for example to remember that a browser check
was passed. Those websites have their own privacy policies; I don't control them and receive nothing from
them.

The app may also download source definitions from repository addresses that you add.

## Narration

Text-to-speech happens on your device, using Apple's voices or voices bundled with the app. The text being
read is not sent to me or to any speech service. Listening position is stored on your device like reading
progress.

## Diagnostics

The app keeps a log of errors and, through Apple's MetricKit, receives crash and hang reports about the
app from iOS. These stay **on your device**. They leave it only if you choose to share them, for example
with "Send a Problem Report" or "Share Crash Reports" in Settings › Diagnostics; then you pick where they
go. They contain technical details (app and iOS version, device model, error messages, code locations),
not your library or reading history, though an error message can mention a source or a novel.

Separately, if you turned on "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics
& Improvements, Apple may share crash and usage statistics about the app with me through App Store
Connect. That is controlled by Apple's settings and privacy policy.

## Purchases (when available)

If the app offers optional paid features, purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the
App Store. I never receive your payment details. The app checks what you have purchased on your device
using Apple's StoreKit.

## Advertising (if it is added)

The app currently shows no ads. If a future version of the App Store edition shows ads, an advertising
network (for example Google AdMob) would receive information such as your IP address and device
information to show and measure ads. Before that happens, this policy and the App Store privacy label will
be updated, and the app will ask for any permission the law or Apple requires (for example App Tracking
Transparency, and consent where required in the EU/UK). Paying to remove ads, where offered, turns ads
off.

## Children

TachiNovel is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from
anyone.

## Your choices

- Clear downloads, caches and logs in Settings › Storage.
- Delete the app to delete everything it stored on the device. Data in your iCloud can be removed in iOS
  Settings › [your name] › iCloud.
- Stop narration, notifications or background refresh at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Because I don't hold data about you, there is nothing for me to export or delete on request, but you can
contact me with any question.

## Security

Data on the device is protected by iOS (your passcode and its encryption). Connections to websites use
HTTPS where the website supports it.

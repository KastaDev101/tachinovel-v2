### Added

- Settings › Voices › Voice packs: several Nephis voice packs (.tnmodel) stay on the iPhone side by side. The list shows
  each pack's name, when it was made, whether it has her studio and its size, with a ✓ on the one she reads with; tap
  one to switch her to it from her next sentence, Edit to delete one (deleting hers switches her to the next pack). The
  total space they use is at the bottom. Opening a pack adds it and makes it hers; the pack already on the phone moves
  into the list at the first launch, nothing to download again.

### Added

- The reader keeps up with the narration. When you come back to the app after listening with the phone locked or
  in the car, it jumps to the sentence being read (switching to the next chapter if narration went on) and marks
  it briefly. While you listen with the screen on, scrolling the page yourself stops the reader from following;
  a "Back to narration" pill brings it back.
